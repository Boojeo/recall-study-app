Tip: cards you write yourself stick better (the generation effect). Use AI cards as a starting point and edit them in your own words.

Gemini asks deeper questions about cards you've already studied: why, how, apply it, compare. Explaining in your own words is one of the strongest ways to make knowledge stick.
